Human-in-the-Loop Safety

Dry run mode is the hard default for every account. The agent stops at AWAITING_REVIEW with visual screenshots and per-field confidence rationales. No silent submissions.

🌐

Multi-Board Aggregation

Direct adapters for Greenhouse, Lever, Remotive, and RemoteOK. Complies with robots.txt, respects crawl-delay limits, and deduplicates listings with SHA-256 fingerprints.

📄

Hardened Resume Parser

Multi-column PDF and DOCX parsing with layout awareness, scanned image detection, non-English script filtering, and automatic skill & experience extraction.

🔒

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Full user authentication with bcrypt password hashing and JWT access tokens. Resumes, profiles, screenshots, and review queues are strictly isolated per account.

How AutoApply AI Works

STEP 1

Upload Resume & Set Goals

Upload your resume to automatically extract your skills, or customize your preferred roles, locations, and remote preferences anytime in your Profile.

STEP 2

Agent Sources & Fills Forms

The Playwright agent crawls targeted boards, matches high-relevance openings, maps form fields with explainable confidence, and takes proof screenshots.

STEP 3

Inspect & Approve

Review visual screenshots and field mapping rationales in your Human Review Queue. Approve to simulate (dry run) or auto-submit on a per-job basis.
